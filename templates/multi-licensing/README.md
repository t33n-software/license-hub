# Multi-licensing (Family G)

This family directory intentionally carries no license-text templates.

Family G is the combination meta-layer of the licensing taxonomy: dual- and
multi-licensing, license stacking, SPDX expressions, license exceptions, and
inbound contribution mechanisms own no license texts of their own. A
composition from this family is served by the member templates of the other
families plus the SPDX expression that binds them.

The canonical composition contract — including the dual-licensing control
prerequisite, the choice-versus-obligation discipline, and the expression
syntax rules — lives in
[../../docs/licensing/multi-licensing/README.md](../../docs/licensing/multi-licensing/README.md).
