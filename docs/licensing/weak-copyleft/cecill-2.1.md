# CeCILL 2.1 (`CECILL-2.1`)

Family: A — Open Source, weak copyleft (A2). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[weak-copyleft README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/weak-copyleft/cecill-2.1/CECILL-2.1-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/weak-copyleft/cecill-2.1/CECILL-2.1-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: CEA / CNRS / Inria; OSI-approved; the French/EU-aligned
  GPL-compatible copyleft. Version 2.0 is superseded by 2.1 and carries no
  template.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, copy, modify, and distribute the software, royalty-free, drafted
  for conformity with French and EU law.
- Conditions: copyleft on the software and its derivatives; source
  availability; notices retained.
- Restrictions: the copyleft follows the software; the text is bilingual
  (French and English) with defined precedence.

## Patent position

Express grant with a litigation-termination clause.

## Compatibility

GPL-compatible by design. Answered from the canonical compatibility matrix.
The BSD-like CeCILL-B and LGPL-like CeCILL-C variants exist for other scopes
and carry no template in this inventory.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `CECILL-2.1`. The template body is
byte-identical to the canonical text.
