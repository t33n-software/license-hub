# Microsoft Reciprocal License (`MS-RL`)

Family: A — Open Source, weak copyleft (A2). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[weak-copyleft README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/weak-copyleft/ms-rl/MS-RL-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/weak-copyleft/ms-rl/MS-RL-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: Microsoft; OSI-approved; the reciprocal variant of MS-PL.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: reproduce, modify, distribute, and sublicense the code, royalty-free.
- Conditions: file-level reciprocity — modifications to licensed files must be
  distributed under the MS-RL with source availability; notices retained.
- Restrictions: no trademark rights; the reciprocity is scoped to the licensed
  files, not to separate larger works.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `MS-RL`. The template body is byte-identical
to the canonical text. Its permissive sibling `MS-PL` lives in
[../permissive/ms-pl.md](../permissive/ms-pl.md).
