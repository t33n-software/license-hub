# Apple Public Source License 2.0 (`APSL-2.0`)

Family: A — Open Source, weak copyleft (A2). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[weak-copyleft README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/weak-copyleft/apsl-2.0/APSL-2.0-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/weak-copyleft/apsl-2.0/APSL-2.0-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: Apple; OSI-approved and FSF-free, but tied to the Apple
  ecosystem and not recommended for general reuse outside it.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the covered code, in source
  and binary form, royalty-free.
- Conditions: source availability for covered modifications; notices retained;
  modifications documented.
- Restrictions: the copyleft scopes to the covered code; the license carries
  Apple-specific definitions and an export-control clause.

## Patent position

Express grant with a termination-on-patent-litigation clause.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `APSL-2.0`. Prefer a mainstream weak-copyleft
member (MPL-2.0 or EPL-2.0) for new projects without an Apple ecosystem
binding. The template body is byte-identical to the canonical text.
