# ESA Public License — Weak Copyleft 2.4 (`ESA-PL-weak-copyleft-2.4`)

Family: A — Open Source, weak copyleft (A2). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[weak-copyleft README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/weak-copyleft/esa-pl-weak-copyleft-2.4/ESA-PL-weak-copyleft-2.4-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/weak-copyleft/esa-pl-weak-copyleft-2.4/ESA-PL-weak-copyleft-2.4-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: European Space Agency; the weakly reciprocal variant of the
  ESA Public License 2.4 family.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the software, royalty-free,
  with EU-aligned drafting.
- Conditions: the copyleft is scoped to the licensed modules; modifications of
  those modules remain under the ESA-PL; separate modules keep their own
  terms.
- Restrictions: no trademark rights; module-level reciprocity.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation.

is byte-identical to the canonical text. The permissive and strongly
reciprocal variants live in

and
