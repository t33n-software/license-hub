## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the covered code, in source
  and executable form, royalty-free.
- Conditions: source availability for covered files; modifications remain
  under the ErlPL; notices retained.
- Restrictions: file-scoped reciprocity; larger works may combine under other
  terms for other files.
