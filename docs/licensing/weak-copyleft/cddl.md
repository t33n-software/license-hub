# Common Development and Distribution Licenses (`CDDL-1.0`, `CDDL-1.1`)

Family: A — Open Source, weak copyleft (A2). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[weak-copyleft README](README.md).

## CDDL 1.0 (`CDDL-1.0`)

- Template: [`templates/weak-copyleft/cddl-1.0/CDDL-1.0-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/weak-copyleft/cddl-1.0/CDDL-1.0-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: Sun Microsystems; OSI-approved; the Sun-era file-level
  copyleft.

### Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute, in source and executable
  form, royalty-free, with a contributor patent grant.
- Conditions: source availability for covered files; modifications remain
  under the CDDL; notices retained.
- Restrictions: file-scoped copyleft; larger works may combine under other
  terms for other files.

### Patent position

Express grant with retaliation.

### Compatibility

GPL-incompatible. Answered from the canonical compatibility matrix.

### Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `CDDL-1.0`. The template body is
byte-identical to the canonical text.
