## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the software, royalty-free.
- Conditions: derivative works remain under the LiLiQ-R on distribution, with
  source availability and retained notices.
- Restrictions: the reciprocity follows the software; no trademark rights.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation, following the LiLiQ family patent clause.
