# ESA Public License — Strong Copyleft 2.4 (`ESA-PL-strong-copyleft-2.4`)

Family: A — Open Source, strong copyleft (A3). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[strong-copyleft README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/strong-copyleft/esa-pl-strong-copyleft-2.4/ESA-PL-strong-copyleft-2.4-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/strong-copyleft/esa-pl-strong-copyleft-2.4/ESA-PL-strong-copyleft-2.4-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: European Space Agency; the strongly reciprocal variant of
  the ESA Public License 2.4 family.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the software, royalty-free,
  with EU-aligned drafting.
- Conditions: derivative and combined works remain under the ESA-PL strong
  copyleft on distribution, with source availability.
- Restrictions: the reciprocity follows the work; no trademark rights.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation.
