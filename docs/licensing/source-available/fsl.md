# Functional Source License 1.1 (`FSL-1.1-ALv2`, `FSL-1.1-MIT`)

Family: B — Source-Available, group B2 (Delayed Open Source). **Not
OSI-approved open source.** Canon: [license taxonomy canon](../README.md);
family reference: [source-available README](README.md).

- Templates:
  [`templates/source-available/fsl-1.1-alv2/FSL-1.1-ALv2-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/source-available/fsl-1.1-alv2/FSL-1.1-ALv2-1.0.0.hbs)

  [`templates/source-available/fsl-1.1-mit/FSL-1.1-MIT-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/source-available/fsl-1.1-mit/FSL-1.1-MIT-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: Sentry; a simplified BUSL variant with fixed conversion
  terms — no free parameters.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, copy, modify, and distribute the software for any purpose that
  is not a competing offering.
- Conditions: notices retained.
- Restrictions: non-compete during the two-year window — no offering of the
  software as a competing product or service.

## Conversion semantics

Each copy converts automatically to the named open license two years after its
publication: `FSL-1.1-ALv2` converts to Apache-2.0, `FSL-1.1-MIT` converts to
MIT. The two variants are complete fixed texts; the variant choice is the
tenant's target-license decision and is the only selection to make.

## Patent position

Grant through the conversion target on conversion; the text itself is
patent-silent during the window — state this when advising.

## Compliance classification

Proprietary-grade during the window: legal review before adoption, no
assumption of redistribution or SaaS rights, explicit scanner policy entry.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `FSL-1.1-ALv2` or `FSL-1.1-MIT`. Both
template bodies are byte-identical to their canonical texts.
