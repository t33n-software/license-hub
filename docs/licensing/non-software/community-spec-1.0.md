# Community Specification License 1.0 (`Community-Spec-1.0`)

Family: F — Non-Software, F6 (Standards and Specifications). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[non-software README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/non-software/community-spec-1.0/Community-Spec-1.0-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/non-software/community-spec-1.0/Community-Spec-1.0-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: Joint Development Foundation; the license for community
  specifications and standards documents.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: copy, display, and distribute the specification; prepare derivative
  specifications under the defined rules; implement the specification.
- Conditions: attribution and the license notice accompany copies; derivative
  specifications follow the naming and marking rules.
- Restrictions: the specification's name and marks are reserved;
  implementations are addressed by the specification's own patent policy
  framework.

## Patent position

The text addresses patent commitments of specification contributors through
the specification process; the document license itself is patent-silent for
mere readers.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `Community-Spec-1.0`. The template body is
byte-identical to the canonical text. Organization-bound document notices
(W3C, IETF, ISO) are not general templates and carry none here.
