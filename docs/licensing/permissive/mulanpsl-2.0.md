# Mulan Permissive Software License v2 (`MulanPSL-2.0`)

Family: A — Open Source, permissive (A1). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[permissive README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/permissive/mulanpsl-2.0/MulanPSL-2.0-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/permissive/mulanpsl-2.0/MulanPSL-2.0-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: China Open Source Promotion Union / CEC; OSI-approved.
  Version 1 is superseded by version 2 and carries no template.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: reproduce, use, modify, and distribute contributions, perpetual,
  worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, and irrevocable.
- Conditions: provide recipients with a copy of the license and retain
  copyright, patent, trademark, and disclaimer statements in the software.
- Restrictions: no trademark license; the text is written in Chinese and
  English with equal effect, and the Chinese version prevails on divergence.

## Patent position

Express patent grant with retaliation — patent litigation or enforcement
activity alleging infringement by the software terminates the licensee's
patent rights.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `MulanPSL-2.0`. The template body is
byte-identical to the canonical bilingual text; both how-to-apply sections
(Chinese and English) keep their bracketed placeholder instructions verbatim
because they describe the per-file source header a tenant adds — they are not
render anchors.
