# MirOS Licence (`MirOS`)

Family: A — Open Source, permissive (A1). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[permissive README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/permissive/miros/MirOS-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/permissive/miros/MirOS-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: MirOS project; OSI-approved; a permissive license drafted
  with European copyright law awareness (author's rights, liability limits
  shaped for EU jurisdictions).

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: deal in the work without restriction, including unlimited rights to
  use, publicly perform, distribute, sell, modify, merge, give away, or
  sublicence.
- Conditions: the terms, the disclaimer, and all copyright notices must be
  retained or reproduced in an accompanying document.
- Restrictions: none beyond retention; liability is excluded except for
  malicious intent or gross negligence, tracking EU mandatory liability
  floors.

## Patent position

No grant.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `MirOS`. The template anchors the copyright
line to the `COPYRIGHT_YEAR` and `COPYRIGHT_HOLDER` anchors and drops the
steward email token.
