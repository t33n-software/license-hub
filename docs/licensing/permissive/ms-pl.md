# Microsoft Public License (`MS-PL`)

Family: A — Open Source, permissive (A1). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[permissive README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/permissive/ms-pl/MS-PL-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/permissive/ms-pl/MS-PL-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: Microsoft; OSI-approved; a short permissive license with an
  express patent grant.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: reproduce, modify, distribute, and sublicense the code, in source or
  object form, royalty-free.
- Conditions: retain all copyright, patent, trademark, and attribution
  notices; provide a copy of the license with distributions.
- Restrictions: no trademark rights; the license does not apply to portions of
  a derivative work that are not "Licensed Code"; no sublicensing of the
  license itself beyond its terms.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation — a patent assertion by the licensee against
the code terminates the licensee's patent rights under the license.

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `MS-PL`. The template body is byte-identical
to the canonical text. Its reciprocal sibling `MS-RL` lives in
[../weak-copyleft/ms-rl.md](../weak-copyleft/ms-rl.md).
