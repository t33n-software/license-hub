# ESA Public License — Permissive 2.4 (`ESA-PL-permissive-2.4`)

Family: A — Open Source, permissive (A1). Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[permissive README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/permissive/esa-pl-permissive-2.4/ESA-PL-permissive-2.4-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/permissive/esa-pl-permissive-2.4/ESA-PL-permissive-2.4-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: European Space Agency; the permissive variant of the ESA
  Public License 2.4 family, drafted for EU legal alignment.

## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the software, in source and
  object form, royalty-free.
- Conditions: retain the copyright notice and the license text; mark modified
  versions.
- Restrictions: no trademark rights; EU-aligned warranty and liability
  wording.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation, following the modern EU-aware drafting model.
