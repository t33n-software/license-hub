## Grant, conditions, restrictions

- Grant: use, copy, modify, distribute, and sublicense, royalty-free.
- Conditions: retain the copyright notice and the license text; mark modified
  versions.
- Restrictions: no trademark rights; termination on patent litigation
  instituted by the licensee.

## Patent position

Express grant with retaliation (termination on licensee patent litigation).
