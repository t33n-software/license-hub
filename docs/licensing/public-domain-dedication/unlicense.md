# The Unlicense (`Unlicense`)

Family: E — Public Domain Dedication. Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[public-domain-dedication README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/public-domain-dedication/unlicense/Unlicense-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/public-domain-dedication/unlicense/Unlicense-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: unlicense.org; OSI-approved dedication with a fallback
  permissive grant.

## Instrument mechanics

- **Dedication:** the authors dedicate any and all copyright interest in the
  software to the public domain, for the benefit of the public at large, as an
  overt act of relinquishment in perpetuity.
- **Fallback:** in jurisdictions that recognize copyright laws, the text
  operates as a free and unencumbered grant to copy, modify, publish, use,
  compile, sell, or distribute, for any purpose, commercial or
  non-commercial.
- **Disclaimer:** the software is provided as-is without warranty.

## Patent position

No grant (silent).

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `Unlicense`. In no-waiver jurisdictions the
dedication degrades to its fallback grant — state this when advising
EU-centric projects, where 0BSD is the safer equivalent. The template body is
byte-identical to the canonical text.
