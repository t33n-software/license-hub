# SQLite Blessing (`blessing`)

Family: E — Public Domain Dedication. Canon:
[license taxonomy canon](../README.md); family reference:
[public-domain-dedication README](README.md).

- Template: [`templates/public-domain-dedication/blessing/blessing-1.0.0.hbs`](../../../templates/public-domain-dedication/blessing/blessing-1.0.0.hbs)
- Steward/status: the SQLite project's blessing-style dedication — the author
  disclaims copyright to the source code and replaces the legal notice with a
  blessing.

## Instrument mechanics

- **Dedication:** the author disclaims copyright to the source code.
- **Blessing in place of a legal notice:** "May you do good and not evil. May
  you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others. May you share freely,
  never taking more than you give."
- **Form:** a one-paragraph dedication without conditions and without a
  fallback license clause.

## Patent position

No grant (silent).

## Adoption

Set `SPDX_LICENSE_IDENTIFIER` to `blessing`. The text carries no fallback
clause, so in no-waiver jurisdictions its effect is uncertain — prefer 0BSD
for EU-centric projects. The template body is byte-identical to the canonical
text.
